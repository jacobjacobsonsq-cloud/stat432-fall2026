Question 2: Margin in four dimensions

Let

zᵢ = (𝐱ᵢᵀβ) / ‖β‖₂

Here ‖β‖₂=√{1^2+2^2+(-2)^2+4^2}=5, so f(𝐱ᵢ)=-1+5zᵢ. The decision boundary is therefore at z=0.2.

The closest projected positions are -0.8 (observations 15 and 36) and 1.2 (observations 4 and 33). Each is one unit from 0.2, so the geometric margin is

M = minᵢ |f(𝐱ᵢ)| / ‖β‖₂ = 1

The smallest functional margin is minᵢ yᵢ f(𝐱ᵢ)=5. Dividing the entire score by 5 preserves the boundary and makes this minimum equal to 1. The SVM-normalized coefficients are

β₀* = −0.2, β* = (0.2, 0.4, −0.4, 0.8)ᵀ
